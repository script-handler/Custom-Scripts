AimLab
This replaces Chaos Aim Trainer and RefleX. The viewport is the main element. The two big buttons and the text stats strip are gone. They're replaced by four stat tiles and a 44px icon footer. During a run the footer goes away and Pause and Max move into the viewport corner, so the viewport grows from 368×438 to 368×492.
FORMATS
Compact 400×640, viewport 368 wide, checked at 316 (A14). Max 1280×800 with the HUD overlaid on the edges.
COMPONENTS
AimPanel, AimViewport (all states), AimMax, Target (disc / sphere / hit / ring / ghost), Crosshair (5 styles, code-driven). StatTile and RoundBars are variants inside them.
THEMES
Aurora dark is the base. Aurora light and Cyberpunk HUD are in the last row. The Cyberpunk accent is user-picked, cyan by default.
INPUT
2D hides the OS cursor and draws the crosshair at the pointer. 3D locks the pointer with the crosshair fixed at centre. Esc pauses.
RANKING
Normal posts to the team board. Easy and Hard show “Custom · not ranked” (A9).
SCORING (MOCK)
Chaos Normal is hits × 10. Popups show the streak multiplier used by the other scenarios.

Spawn rules
01A candidate is accepted only if dist(A,B) ≥ rA + rB + gap holds against every live target. Gap is 24 px at Normal. The dashed halos show r + gap/2, so halos may touch but never cross.
02The aim zone stays clear at spawn: the target's edge must be at least 48 px from the crosshair (2D) or 10° from the view ray (3D).
03No spawns within r + 12 px of the edge or under the in-run HUD (Pause / Max).
04A candidate that fails is rejected and redrawn, up to 40 tries. After that the gap relaxes by 25% at a time, but never below 0. Overlap is impossible.
053D uses the same test on a sphere shell around the camera: each target's angular radius is plus 3°, measured from the camera, so depth can't hide an overlap.
06Drift at Hard re-checks every frame. Targets that would close below the gap steer apart instead of passing through each other.
